I study how the microtubule cytoskeleton is regulated in parasites. During my PhD at MIVEGEC (CNRS, IRD, University of Montpellier), I investigated β-tubulin tyrosination, an unusual feature of trypanosomatids, and showed that it tunes tubulin glutamylation and parasite motility in Trypanosoma brucei. My toolkit combines CRISPR/Cas9 functional genetics, expansion microscopy, TurboID proximity labeling and RNA-seq.

Research experience

  1. 2023–2026

    Doctoral researcher

    MIVEGEC (CNRS, IRD, University of Montpellier)

    • Investigated β-tubulin (de)tyrosination and its functions in the microtubule cytoskeleton of Trypanosoma brucei and Leishmania.
    • Showed that β-tubulin tyrosination tunes tubulin glutamylation and parasite motility (first-author preprint, 2026).
    • Combined CRISPR/Cas9 functional genetics, TurboID proximity labeling, iterative expansion microscopy and RNA-seq.
    • Co-authored a study of tubulin detyrosination in Leishmania (PNAS, 2025).
    • Supervised two Master’s students and one engineering student; taught cell biology and designed exam material for Master’s students.
    • Iterative expansion microscopy
    • TurboID
    • CRISPR/Cas9
    • RNA-seq
    • Western blot
    • Supervision
    • Scientific writing

  2. 2022

    Research intern, Master’s level

    Laboratory of Pathogens and Host Immunity (LPHI), University of Montpellier

    Investigated the role of phosphatidylinositol 3-kinase (PI3K) in Plasmodium falciparum using a conditional DiCre knockout approach. Assessed PI3K function in hemoglobin digestion, apicoplast maintenance and artemisinin resistance through parasite culture, transfection, PCR/qPCR, fluorescence microscopy and Western blotting. This work contributed to a Scientific Reports article (2025).

    • qPCR
    • Parasite culture
    • Fluorescence microscopy
    • Conditional knockout

  3. 2022

    CRBM (CNRS), University of Montpellier

    Studied the subcellular localization of toxin–antidote effector proteins of the intracellular bacterium Wolbachia in Drosophila S2R+ cells. Generated plasmid constructs, performed lipofection-based transfections and analyzed protein localization by confocal microscopy.

    • PCR
